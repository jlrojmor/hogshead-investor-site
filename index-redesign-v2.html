Two business models

One platform. Two ways to monetize aged tequila.

The website should immediately split the visitor journey: asset-backed tequila programs for investors and brands, and a product-forward single-barrel program for buyers ready to select, customize, and sell.

FAQ

Clean answers. No over-disclosure.

Do you name distillery partners publicly?

No. Public language should reference premium Mexican distillery partners. Specific partner details belong in direct discussions.

Is this only for accredited investors?

No. The site should serve investors, brands, restaurants, retailers, private buyers, and people exploring tequila opportunities. Specific transactions may have their own requirements.

Can buyers customize the bottle?

Yes. Hogshead can support custom presentation and client logo placement depending on program type.

Where will distribution begin?

The initial U.S. focus is Texas and California, with compliance depending on state-specific rules and channel structure.
