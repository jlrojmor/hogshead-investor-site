Tequila Appreciation

Understanding the aging process and value drivers that create premium pricing.

TIME + SCARCITY = PRICE POWER

Aging Process

Barrel-Only Aging

Tequila only ages in barrel (not in bottle) – value builds while resting. This creates a unique investment opportunity where time directly correlates with value appreciation.

Scarcity Factors

Compounding Scarcity

Scarcity compounds: angel's share, long cycles, limited long-term stock. Each year of aging reduces available inventory while increasing demand for premium aged expressions.

Premium Pricing

Step-Change Pricing

Result: Older, limited releases command step-change pricing. Aged tequila achieves exponential price increases as it becomes increasingly rare and sought after.
