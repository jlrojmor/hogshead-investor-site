Tequila aging journey

From clean agave to scarce aged expression.

Tequila only builds aging value while resting in barrel. Category thresholds create natural step changes: Blanco, Reposado, Añejo, Extra Añejo, and long-aged specialty releases.

Blanco

0–2 months. Clean agave base before meaningful barrel influence.

Reposado

At least 2 months. Early oak, vanilla, spice, and softened edges.

Añejo

At least 12 months. Deeper oak, caramel, dried fruit, and complexity.

Extra Añejo

At least 36 months. Scarcity, structure, depth, and premium positioning.

FAQ

Clear answers for first conversations.

Is Hogshead only for accredited investors?

No. The website is designed for a broader audience: investors, brands, restaurants, bars, retailers, private buyers, and people exploring tequila opportunities. Specific transactions may have their own requirements.

Do you disclose the exact legal structure online?

No. The public website should explain the concept at a high level. Specific legal, custody, and documentation mechanics should be handled in direct conversations and formal materials.

Do you work with one distillery?

The public language should say Hogshead works with premium Mexican distillery partners. We do not need to name individual partners on the website.

Can restaurants or liquor stores buy single barrel releases?

Yes, depending on the buyer profile, state rules, distributor channel, and compliance requirements. The website should invite inquiries without over-explaining state-by-state rules.
